GNM course in Sundargarh

General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) is a three-year diploma course. You learn to care for patients in hospitals and in the community, and to look after mothers and newborn babies. Classes are combined with practical training on hospital wards.

  1. Year 1

    The body and basic care

    Anatomy and physiology, microbiology, psychology, sociology, fundamentals of nursing, first aid, community health and nutrition.

  2. Year 2

    Nursing on the wards

    Medical-surgical nursing, mental health nursing and child health nursing, with clinical postings in hospital.

  3. Year 3

    Midwifery and community health

    Midwifery and gynaecological nursing, community health nursing, and a supervised internship.

Where GNM can take you

After passing GNM you can register as a nurse and midwife and start working.

  • Hospitals

    Work as a staff nurse in government and private hospitals, nursing homes and clinics.

  • Community health

    Serve in health centres and public health programmes, caring for families in towns and villages.

  • Further study

    Go on to courses such as Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing and move into senior nursing or teaching roles.

Questions about GNM admission

What is the eligibility for GNM at Gayatri College of Nursing?

You need to pass +2 in any stream with at least 40% marks (General) or 35% marks (SC/ST). Your age should be between 17 and 40 years.

Can Arts and Commerce students apply for GNM?

Yes. Students who passed +2 in any stream, including Arts, Commerce and Science, can apply for GNM at Gayatri College of Nursing.

Can boys apply for the GNM course?

Yes. Both boys and girls can apply.

How long is the GNM course?

GNM (General Nursing and Midwifery) is a 3-year course.

Is hostel available?
